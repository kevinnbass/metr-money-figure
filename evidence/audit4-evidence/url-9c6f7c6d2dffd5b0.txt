Return Reference Explanation PART X, LINE 2: NATIONAL PHILANTHROPIC TRUST AND AFFILIATES (ORGANIZATIONS") ARE GENERALLY EXEMPT FROM FEDERAL INCOME TAXES UNDER THE PROVISIONS OF SECTION 501(C)(3) OF THE CODE. IN ADDITION, THE ORGANIZATIONS QUALIFY FOR CHARITABLE CONTRIBUTION DEDUCTIONS AND HAVE BEEN CLASSIFIED AS ORGANIZATIONS THAT ARE NOT PRIVATE FOUNDATIONS. THE ORGANIZATIONS ARE SUBJECT TO INCOME TAXES ON NET INCOME THAT IS DERIVED FROM A TRADE OR BUSINESS, REGULARLY CARRIED ON, AND NOT IN FURTHERANCE OF THE PURPOSES FOR WHICH IT WAS GRANTED EXEMPTION. THE ORGANIZATIONS' AGGREGATE OBLIGATION FOR UNRELATED BUSINESS INCOME TAX FOR THE YEARS ENDED JUNE 30, 2025 AND 2024, WAS APPROXIMATELY $778,000 AND $436,000, RESPECTIVELY. THE ORGANIZATIONS FILE U.S., UK AND STATE INCOME TAX RETURNS IN JURISDICTIONS, AS APPLICABLE, TO REPORT SUCH INCOME. THE ORGANIZATIONS FOLLOW THE ACCOUNTING GUIDANCE ON ACCOUNTING FOR UNCERTAINTY IN INCOME TAXES (FINANCIAL ACCOUNTING STANDARDS BOARD (FASB) ASC TOPIC 740-10), WHICH ADDRESSES THE DETERMINATION OF WHETHER TAX BENEFITS CLAIMED OR EXPECTED TO BE CLAIMED ON A TAX RETURN SHOULD BE RECORDED ON THE CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS. UNDER THIS GUIDANCE, THE ORGANIZATIONS MAY RECOGNIZE THE TAX BENEFIT FROM AN UNCERTAIN TAX POSITION ONLY IF IT IS MORE LIKELY THAN NOT THAT THE TAX POSITION WILL BE SUSTAINED ON EXAMINATION BY A TAX AUTHORITY. THE TAX BENEFITS RECOGNIZED IN THE CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FROM SUCH A POSITION ARE MEASURED BASED ON THE LARGEST BENEFIT THAT HAS A GREATER THAN 50% LIKELIHOOD OF BEING REALIZED UPON ULTIMATE SETTLEMENT. THE GUIDANCE ON ACCOUNTING FOR UNCERTAINTY IN INCOME TAXES ALSO ADDRESSES DERECOGNITION, CLASSIFICATION, INTEREST AND PENALTIES ON INCOME TAXES, AND ACCOUNTING IN INTERIM PERIODS. MANAGEMENT EVALUATED THE ORGANIZATIONS' TAX POSITIONS AND CONCLUDED THAT THE ORGANIZATIONS HAVE TAKEN NO UNCERTAIN TAX POSITIONS THAT REQUIRE ADJUSTMENT TO THE CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS TO COMPLY WITH THE PROVISIONS OF THIS GUIDANCE. NO MATERIAL ACCRUAL FOR INTEREST AND PENALTIES WAS DEEMED NECESSARY FOR THE YEARS ENDED JUNE 30, 2025 AND 2024. THE ORGANIZATIONS FILE INFORMATIONAL TAX RETURNS IN U.S. AND UK JURISDICTIONS.
