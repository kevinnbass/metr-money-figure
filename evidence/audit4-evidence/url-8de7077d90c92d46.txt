He completed his PhD in artificial intelligence at UC Berkeley, advised by Stuart Russell . His goal is to develop techniques necessary for advanced automated systems to verifiably act according to human preferences, even in situations unanticipated by their designer. His past work has shown that even superhuman AI systems are vulnerable to adversarial strategies, and explored fundamental limitations of value learning methods. For more information, visit his website . METR (pronounced 'meter') is a research nonprofit that scientifically measures whether and when AI systems might threaten catastrophic harm to society.
