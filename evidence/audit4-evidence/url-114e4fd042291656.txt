Outside of FAR.AI, I am a board member of the Safe AI Forum , the London Initiative for Safe AI and METR . Prior to founding FAR.AI, I received my PhD from UC Berkeley under the supervision of Stuart Russell , and previously worked at Google DeepMind with Jan Leike and Geoffrey Irving . Please see my CV for a more comprehensive list of my prior experience.
