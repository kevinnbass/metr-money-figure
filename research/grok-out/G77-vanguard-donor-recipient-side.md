# G77 — Who is behind Vanguard Charitable's $4.0M to METR: the recipient side and portfolio matching (web)

Vanguard Charitable (a donor-advised-fund sponsor) granted METR $4,000,000 in FY2025 (Jul 2024–Jun 2025), ARC $1.5M, and a cluster of AI-safety/EA organizations $65.7M in total that year, up from $8.4M the year before (research/vanguard-ai-cluster.csv; research/NOTES-vanguard.md). The sponsor names no advisor. FY2025 tier-A lines: Convergent Research $25.1M; Transluce/Clarity AI $6.585M; Giving What We Can USA $5.25M; Horizon Institute $4.22M; METR $4.0M; Sentinel Bio $3.35M; Legal Priorities/LawAI $2.7M; Longview USA $2.5M; GovAI $2.0M; Rethink Priorities $1.6M; ARC $1.5M; Founders Pledge $1.21M; CAIS $1.08M; Navigation Charitable Fund $1.075M; Open Philanthropy Advisors $1.0M; plus Epoch, AIFP, Lightcone, MATS.

Task A — recipient acknowledgments: for every organization above, fetch its donor/supporter/funders page, annual report, 990 Schedule B (if a 990-PF or voluntarily disclosed), impact report, and any 'thank you to our funders' post for 2024–2026. Record every named donor and every fund named in the form '<Name> Fund', '<Name> Family Foundation', 'a donor-advised fund at Vanguard Charitable', 'Vanguard Charitable' or 'anonymous DAF'. Sentinel Bio's page is known to list individuals (Sam Bowman, Cullen O'Keefe, Max Henderson, Brian Chen, Craig Falls) plus DAFs; do the same for all.
Task B — intersection: build the table recipient × named donor; list every donor or fund that appears on three or more of the FY2025 tier-A recipients' acknowledgments. Those are the candidates for the Vanguard account(s).
Task C — portfolio matching against public grant lists: Navigation Fund (navigation.org grants; Jed McCaleb), Schmidt Sciences, Founders Pledge's public grants, Longview's, Lightspeed Grants, Manifund regrantors, EA Funds (LTFF) payouts, Survival and Flourishing Fund, the Coefficient index, and any individual donor's public ledger (Tallinn's is known). For each list, mark which FY2025 tier-A recipients it funds and in what year; report the lists whose recipient set most resembles the Vanguard FY2025 set. Specifically test the McCaleb hypothesis: does Navigation Fund or McCaleb personally publicly fund Convergent Research, Transluce, Horizon, METR, GovAI, LawAI, Sentinel Bio? Quote each.
Task D — Convergent Research $25.1M: Convergent's funders page and press for its FRO programme 2024–26 (Schmidt, Open Phil/Coefficient, Navigation Fund, Astera, others); any statement of a $25M-scale gift in that window; whether any funder is described as giving through a DAF.
Task E — METR's own acknowledgments: metr.org/about supporters list (current and Wayback captures 2024–2026), METR's Aug 14 2026 funding update, METR's 2025 annual letter if any, and any METR post thanking a named fund; Beth Barnes's LessWrong/EA Forum posts naming donors.
