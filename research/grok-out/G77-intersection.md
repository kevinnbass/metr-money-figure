# G77 — Vanguard Charitable’s FY2025 AI-safety cluster: recipient-side intersections

## Finding

**The public record does not identify the adviser—or advisers—behind Vanguard Charitable’s FY2025 grants to METR or the wider tier-A cluster.** Vanguard’s filing reports a fiscal-year total by recipient and does not expose the recommending account. It therefore does not establish that the $4.0 million METR total, the $25.1 million Convergent total, or the other lines came from one adviser.

Recipient acknowledgments produce six names that appear at three or more tier-A recipients: Coefficient Giving/Open Philanthropy, the Survival and Flourishing Fund (SFF), Longview Philanthropy, Schmidt Sciences/Eric and Wendy Schmidt, Navigation Fund, and Good Ventures. They are useful portfolio signals. None supplies a public Vanguard-account link.

The broadest public portfolio matches are:

1. **Jaan Tallinn’s disclosed ledger: 14 of 19 recipients**
2. **Coefficient Giving’s grant index: 13 of 19**
3. **SFF’s published recommendations: 12 of 19**

Those high overlaps show that the Vanguard cluster sits inside a densely co-funded AI-safety/effective-altruism ecosystem. Tallinn’s disclosed payments also name routes such as Founders Pledge, SFF/Silicon Valley Community Foundation, and Lightspeed Grants, which cuts against silently relabeling those known gifts as Vanguard grants.

## Scope and method

The 19 FY2025 tier-A recipients are Convergent Research, Transluce/Clarity AI, Giving What We Can USA, Horizon Institute, METR, Sentinel Bio, LawAI/Legal Priorities, Longview USA, GovAI, Rethink Priorities, ARC, Founders Pledge, CAIS, Navigation Fund, Open Philanthropy Advisors, Epoch, AI Futures Project, Lightcone, and MATS.

I checked recipient supporter/funder pages, annual or impact reports, public filings, and 2024–2026 thank-you/funding posts. I then compared the resulting names with public portfolios from Navigation, Schmidt, Founders Pledge, Longview, Lightspeed, Manifund, EA Funds/LTFF, SFF, Coefficient, and Tallinn. The companion [CSV](./G77-vanguard-donor.csv) contains 317 source and synthesis rows with verbatim excerpts, URLs, and a checked_utc on every row.

“Appears” below means the recipient itself named the funder or donor. It does not mean the named party advised a Vanguard DAF.

### Schedule B result

None of these public charities voluntarily exposed an unredacted contributor list for the requested window. Public copies of a public charity’s Schedule B generally do not disclose contributor identities. That absence means recipient 990s cannot bridge the acknowledgments to a Vanguard advising account.

### Ranked intersection leads

This ranking orders research leads, not identified account holders.

1. **Coefficient Giving / Open Philanthropy.** Eight recipient acknowledgments and 13 of 19 names in the public index. Against: Coefficient’s 2025 donor-suggestions page explicitly says [METR “is not a Coefficient Giving grantee”](https://coefficientgiving.org/research/suggestions-for-individual-donors-from-coefficient-giving-staff-2025/). Its recommendations are paid through multiple vehicles.

2. **Survival and Flourishing Fund.** Seven acknowledgments and 12 of 19 names in [SFF’s recommendations](https://survivalandflourishing.fund/). Against: SFF identifies Jaan Tallinn, and his [ledger](https://jaan.info/philanthropy/donations.csv) discloses SFF/SVCF and Founders Pledge routes for known gifts, not Vanguard.

3. **Longview Philanthropy.** METR, GovAI, and Rethink acknowledge it; Longview features [METR as an AI grant](https://www.longview.org/artificial-intelligence/). Against: underlying donors are often private and the Frontier AI Fund report is not public.

4. **Schmidt Sciences / Eric and Wendy Schmidt.** Convergent, METR, and Epoch name Schmidt support. Against: the Schmidt awardee directory and 2024 foundation filing do not list a METR organization grant; no source says Schmidt used Vanguard.

5. **Navigation Fund / Jed McCaleb.** Convergent, Horizon, and Rethink name Navigation; Navigation says McCaleb supplied its founding endowment. Vanguard also granted Navigation $1.075 million in FY2025. Against: the seven-recipient test below finds Navigation at only Convergent and Horizon, and a Vanguard grant to Navigation does not identify the adviser.

6. **Good Ventures.** Convergent and MATS name it, and Coefficient/OPA calls it its most significant partner. Against: METR is not a Coefficient grantee, and no source mentions a Vanguard route.

METR’s “individuals from Jane Street” class remains directly relevant but supplies no names. METR’s named individuals—David Farhi, Geoff Ralston, Dylan Field, and Steve Newman—do not meet the three-recipient threshold, and no reviewed source ties any of them to Vanguard.

## Task D — Convergent’s $25.1 million Vanguard total

- Convergent’s [funder page](https://www.convergentresearch.org/about) names Coefficient, Eric and Wendy Schmidt, Good Ventures, Navigation, SFF, and many others. Its almost-$400 million figure covers Convergent and nearly a dozen FROs since 2021 and is not allocated by donor.

- Coefficient’s index shows Convergent grants of $250,000 in 2022, $400,000 in 2023, $1.1 million in 2025, and a $5 million formal-verification project in 2026. They do not explain $25.1 million.

- Eric Schmidt and Ken Griffin announced a [$50 million commitment](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alexknapp/2023/03/17/why-billionaires-ken-griffin-and-eric-schmidt-are-spending-50-million-on-a-new-kind-of-scientific-research/) in March 2023, outside the requested gift window. Forest Neurotech says [Eric and Wendy Schmidt supported it in March 2024](https://forestneurotech.org/fro-advantage), with Griffin Catalyst, Riley & Susan Bechtel Foundation, and James Fickel, but gives no amount.

- **No single gift of roughly $25 million in 2024–2026 was found. No retrieved source describes a named Convergent funder as giving through a DAF.**

- **Astera:** no Astera name was found on the current funder page or retrieved 2024–2026 Convergent/FRO press. Halcyon Futures is named and was not treated as Astera.

The filing’s $25.1 million line can itself combine several Vanguard accounts, so amount matching cannot identify one donor.

## Conclusion

The intersection work narrows the ecosystem but does not identify the Vanguard account. Coefficient, SFF/Tallinn, Longview, Schmidt, Navigation/McCaleb, and Good Ventures all have evidence for overlap and specific evidence against confident attribution.

> **One or more undisclosed Vanguard Charitable DAF advisers funded METR and the other recipients; public evidence does not show whether the FY2025 cluster came from one account or several.**

A named-person attribution would require a recipient disclosure, donor statement, or Vanguard account-level record that is absent from the sources reviewed here.
